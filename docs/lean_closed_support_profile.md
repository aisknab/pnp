## Exact scope

For arbitrary finite input, ordinary-output and computational-field widths, the actual computed dependency-closed support observes a field exactly when it retains an original constant, input or gate source whose value agrees with that field uniformly over every input valuation. The matching table and support are computed from the circuit and ordinary seed data. For arbitrary seed pairs, observations of the computed union are the Boolean union of the separate observations. This extends to arbitrary finite families, with the empty support's observation as the base, and to seed-inclusion monotonicity. Seeding the requested profile records derives availability and uniform field-value preservation without injecting their literal original gate bindings. The actual meet, left, right and join of the constructed support square all preserve those requested computational fields and agree pairwise on their values at every ambient input valuation. Existing source extraction, structural square laws and arbitrary-context profile locality are reused, not newly claimed.

## Limits
