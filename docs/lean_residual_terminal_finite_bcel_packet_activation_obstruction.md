# Finite BCEL/Packet activation-coherence obstruction

`PNP.TerminalFiniteBCELPacketActivationCoherent` names the exact missing
numerical compatibility condition between the M183 finite BCEL-ready nucleus
and the same grouped Packet family bound to it by M184. The condition is
checked over the complete canonical finite cut domain; it is not accepted from
a caller-supplied flag.

## What is checked

For one `TerminalFiniteBCELPacketCarrierCoherenceCertificate`, the terminal
defect is computed from the exact M183 anchor problem and ready nucleus. The
Boolean checker requires:

- the supplied Packet-family cut value to equal that terminal defect; and
- every nonempty proper sublist of the exact M184 Packet carrier to have
  activation weight equal to the same defect.

`checkTerminalFiniteBCELPacketActivationCoherence_eq_true_iff` proves exact
reflection. The proper-cut list is obtained by filtering the complete
canonical sublist enumeration, rather than sampling a few cuts.

If the checker hypothetically accepted, the M184 carrier equality maps every
proper terminal anchor cut to a Packet cut with that activation weight. The
M183 constant-cut theorem identifies the same value with the cut's projection
excess. Thus
`activation_coherent_mapped_cut_equation` reconstructs the precise mapped-cut
equation required by the proposed bridge.

The already checked same-family Packet exclusion in M184 rules out constant
activation. Consequently `not_activation_coherent` proves that the complete
coherence condition cannot hold for the supplied data. The deterministic
classifier returns either:

- a proof that the declared Packet cut value differs from the terminal defect;
  or
- the first canonical nonempty proper Packet cut whose activation weight
  differs from that defect, with its sublist and properness proofs.

`PNP.terminal_finite_bcel_packet_activation_obstruction_checked_complete` and
the report-facing `PNP.zeroslack_bcel_packet_activation_obstruction_checked_complete`
expose that proof-bearing obstruction without rerunning the upstream
classifiers.

## Claim boundary

This is a diagnostic obstruction, not a proof of activation coherence. It
localizes the missing equality on the exact same family and provides a
reproducible counterexample branch for any supplied certificate. It does not
repair the incompatibility or derive Packet activation data from terminal
projection data.

The terminal problem, positive premise, grouped family, anchor map, activation
weights, payloads, budget data, realizer and dependency tables, and rank maps
remain supplied. Complete sublist enumeration may be exponential. This result
does not establish manuscript-wide `SaturatePositive` or `BCELReady`,
unconditional `ZeroSlack`, an exact polynomial PCCMin algorithm, polynomial
runtime, `CNFSAT ∈ P`, removal of a project-specific axiom, the root theorem,
or `P = NP`.

M185 therefore adds one formal-publication evidence row but closes no fixed
risk-weighted progress checkpoint. The proof-completion estimate remains 30
percent with its 20-to-40-percent uncertainty range.
