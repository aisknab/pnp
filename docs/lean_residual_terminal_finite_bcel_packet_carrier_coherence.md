# Same-candidate finite BCEL-ready and Packet carrier coherence

`PNP.TerminalFiniteBCELPacketCarrierCoherenceCertificate` closes one checked
dependency edge between two previously formalized finite boundaries. It binds
the exact successful finite BCEL-ready nucleus from M183 to the exact grouped
BN6 Packet family already consumed by the M180 Packet/budget no-lower
certificate and by the report-facing ZeroSlack record.

## What is checked

The certificate is indexed by one
`PacketBudgetNoLowerZeroSlackSidecarCertificate`. Its terminal problem is
therefore definitionally over that certificate's candidate and executable
saturation model. It retains the existing
`TerminalFiniteBCELReadyCertificate` for that problem, together with:

- a map from terminal primitive-record anchors to the Packet family's anchor
  representation;
- proofs that the map is injective and surjective; and
- a Boolean-reflected exact list equality identifying the Packet carrier with
  the map image of the computed ready nucleus.

`family_carrier_eq` reflects that equality from decidable data. Because list
mapping preserves length, `carrier_at_least_two` transfers M183's nontrivial
nucleus bound without accepting a second carrier-size Boolean. The exact M180
certificate supplies `no_positive_packet`; the arbitrary-finite BN6 theorem
then turns any constant-activation equation on that same family into the
excluded positive Packet.

The named endpoint
`PNP.terminal_finite_bcel_packet_carrier_coherent_checked_complete` exposes the
safe terminal trace, positive final slack, positive whole-support projection
defect, injective and surjective anchor map, exact mapped-carrier identity,
nontrivial carrier, positive-Packet exclusion, and constant-activation
exclusion together.

## Report-facing use

`ZeroSlackCertificate` now stores this coherence certificate rather than the
older independent `BCELContradictionCertificate`. Its current soundness
boundary and Selector/HB/Packet endpoint consequently use the same mapped
family. The older theorem remains part of the historical M181 boundary; it is
not presented as the current report-facing dependency.

## Claim boundary

The terminal problem, initial positive full-slack premise, grouped Packet
family, anchor bijection, payloads, budget, typed realizer table, dependency
table, and rank maps remain supplied proof-bearing inputs. The carrier identity
does not identify Packet-family activation weights with proper-cut projection
excess, nor derive constant activation from positive residual slack. It does
not construct BN3--BN6 data from the terminal nucleus, complete the no-lower
ledger, prove unconditional ZeroSlack or PCCMin, establish polynomial runtime,
put SAT in P, discharge a project assumption, or prove `P = NP`.
