# Terminal Packet-budget no-lower composition

`PNP.DirectWire.checkTerminalPacketBudgetNoLowerComposition` joins two
independently reflected finite no-lower boundaries over one direct-wire
candidate:

- the complete canonical terminal budget-support ledger; and
- the five-row checked Packet ledger for one supplied grouped family, typed
  realizer table, dependency table, and rank functions.

The checker does not accept caller-supplied component results. It recomputes
both component Booleans and accepts only their conjunction. Its reflection
theorem exposes the exact conjunction of the two component propositions.

The endpoint
`terminal_packet_budget_no_lower_composition_excludes_gain_and_packet`
therefore proves three statements together: every governed budget-feasible
canonical support is a semantic minimum, no such support has a strict
equivalent gain, and the supplied Packet family has no positive Packet
conclusion.

This is a finite two-branch composition. The budget caps, Packet family,
typed payloads, ranks, realizer claims, activity environment, and dependency
rows remain supplied. The two branches share the current candidate, but this
milestone does not construct the Packet data from its terminal model or prove
that these are all manuscript no-lower branches. It does not implement HN/BUD
grammar, polynomial HResolve or BudgetResolve, normalization, remaining named
routes, saturation, replay, unconditional ZeroSlack, polynomial PCCMin, SAT in
P, or `P = NP`.
