## Computed operations and complete histories

Raw swaps are checked against the carrier at the operation, including after
earlier replacements shrink it. Literal field sources and arbitrary causal
labels follow the actual emitted wiring. Reordering retains the exact pending
snapshot function and creates or discharges no obligation.

Each new physical gate position maps back through the actual compiler
bijection. This local operation allocates and removes nothing. The complete
program lifts those local origins through its previous ledger, so earlier
allocations keep their historical identities and charges even after a reorder.
No caller-supplied ownership, ordering or correctness witness is accepted.

The expanded certificate checker still rejects incomplete programs, failed
tails, improper whole supports, an open final ledger and a lack of strict saving.
A successful reorder alone is not a smaller equivalent circuit.
