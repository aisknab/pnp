## Actual program and complete observations

The inputs are an actual finite `WireCarrier inputs outputs fields` and a Boolean
keep mask over its ordered computational field coordinates. The mask is supplied
as data; deriving a global route or projection is not claimed. No observer,
materializer, discharge trace or correctness certificate is supplied to the
restoration constructor.

The projected word retains every ordinary output and kept wire field, pads
forgotten coordinates with quotient-only constants, and uses the existing
physical normalizer. Quotient padding is not full-field agreement. The separate
materializer starts from the original literal program and all forgotten field
sources, with no ordinary outputs, then uses the same normalizer.

Literal common-input concatenation joins those two actual programs. Ordinary
outputs and kept fields come from the prefix; forgotten fields come from the
materializer suffix. General theorems preserve every ordinary output and every
ordered field for every valuation and every finite dimension. No assumed
restoration equality appears in their inputs.

The physical charge is exactly the projected gate count plus the materializer
gate count. A shared materializer is paid once, not once per field reference.
This construction need not be a net gain or a semantic minimum. Its gate count
is at most twice the original gate count; this is an output-size fact, not a
total encoded-size polynomial execution or certificate-size theorem. The
`gain?` result requires a strict decrease after paying the full restoration
cost, not merely a saving in the projected program.

## Derived lost-wire lifecycle

Each actually forgotten coordinate receives an R5 record bound to its original
literal source. Its computed R8 discharge names the actual restored source and
contains an all-valuation full-value witness. A record is not a new Boolean
source, and a quotient equality cannot substitute for that witness.

The generator enumerates precisely the forgotten coordinates, each once,
with creation followed by its full discharge. The generated ledger closes.
Replay checks creation identities across the whole trace, including initially
pending identities: an ID cannot be reused after it has been discharged.
The pending-stack check also rejects orphan discharges and incorrect order.

This is the computational R5/full-R8 slice only. It is not the complete
finite-kernel rewrite semantics, R6/R7 cancellation or an arbitrary
obligation-dependency DAG checker. The general full carrier, noncomputational
record histories, full Pull/Expand materializer calculus, complete Package E
and N1-N10 remain open.

duplicate physical gates, all-kept and all-forgotten masks, empty dimensions,
primary-input and constant fields, reordered observations, projected savings
with nonzero restoration cost, and rejected orphan, duplicate, reused and
out-of-order identities. Runtime execution is test evidence, not theorem
