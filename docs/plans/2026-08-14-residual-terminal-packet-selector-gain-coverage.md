# Residual terminal Packet selector gain-coverage plan

## Legacy anchor

Pinned manuscript Sections 14 and 15 require selector silence to range over
every faithful compatible selector capable of realizing a strict equivalent
gain. The preceding milestone exhausts only the canonical handles and original
payloads of one supplied finite grouped BN6 family.

## Unbounded abstraction

Work over every atom type, direct-wire interface, current implementation, and
arbitrary finite explicit grouped BN6 family. Introduce a proof-bearing premise
that every strict equivalent gain is the payload of an original atom in an
exact canonical selector source cell. Compose that premise with the existing
exhaustive finite scan without fixing a carrier cardinality, family size,
candidate-list length, circuit arity, or gate count.

## Exact theorem boundary

The certified scan has only two outcomes:

- a canonical selector handle and original atom in its exact source cell,
  together with a kernel-checked strict equivalent gain and strict residual
  descent; or
- a proof-bearing `ZeroSlackResult`, obtained because exhaustive source-atom
  no-gain and the explicit certificate jointly rule out every strict equivalent
  implementation.

The pair, positive balanced-triple, and full-span Packet alternatives remain
literal fields of the composed conclusion. An empty-family positive-slack
regression proves that finite-family silence alone cannot manufacture the
coverage premise.

## Remaining downstream blockers

The coverage certificate, grouped family, and candidate implementations remain
explicit inputs. The milestone does not construct or polynomially enumerate a
faithful compatible selector universe, derive coverage from terminal data,
construct replacement circuits, establish encoded-size bounds, turn failures
into typed blockers, close the HB/rank argument, or prove unconditional
ZeroSlack or PCCMin. It does not put CNF-SAT in P, remove a project assumption,
or prove `P = NP`.

## Required evidence

- Lean theorem module over arbitrary finite grouped families and circuit
  arities.
- Source-derived axiom audit and generic regression covering both certified
  outcomes, strict descent, ZeroSlack soundness, every Packet branch, and the
  empty-family non-inference counterexample.
- Hostile JavaScript audit rejecting weakened coverage quantification, invented
  payloads, proof-free ZeroSlack, bypassed exhaustive scans, fixed bounds,
  erased Packet branches, and unconditional overclaims.
- Synchronized theorem inventory, publication map, status payloads, report,
  documentation, and durable read-only workflow checks.
