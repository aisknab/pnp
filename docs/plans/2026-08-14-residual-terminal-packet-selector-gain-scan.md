# Residual terminal Packet selector checked-gain scan plan

## Legacy anchor

Pinned manuscript Section 14 permits a selector realizer to expose a gain only
after the replacement candidate passes `VerifyDW`. The preceding milestone
materializes an original positive payload from a decoded Packet selector, but
does not interpret or verify candidate implementations carried by that payload.

## Unbounded abstraction

Work over every arbitrary finite explicit grouped BN6 family, every atom type,
and every direct-wire input/output arity. Specialize only the payload type to
candidate implementations. Decode the existing canonical selector code, select
the exact original grouped cell, and scan all candidate payloads in that cell
with the existing executable strict-equivalent-gain checker. Do not fix a
carrier cardinality, selector count, candidate-list length, or circuit arity.

## Exact theorem boundary

Define the exact candidate list carried by a decoded selector and a total scan
whose only outcomes are:

- an original source-cell payload atom together with a kernel-checked
  `StrictEquivalentGain`; or
- proof that no candidate payload in that selected source cell is a strict
  equivalent gain.

Lift this scan through the fail-closed canonical decoder, prove that decoder
acceptance is exactly scan availability, retain canonical input recovery and
exact source-footprint membership, prove strict residual descent for every gain
outcome, and preserve the pair, positive balanced-triple, and full-span Packet
alternatives with one checked local scan at every selected footprint.

## Remaining downstream blockers

The candidate implementations and grouped BN6 family remain explicit input
data. A local no-gain result excludes only payload candidates in one selected
cell; it is not a manuscript `BotHN`, `BotBUD`, or lower-rank `BotSeed`, and it
does not imply global minimality or ZeroSlack. The milestone does not construct
candidate replacements, prove selector faithfulness or compatibility, connect
payload mass to charge surplus, derive or group BN6 survivors from a terminal
candidate, bound the selector family by encoded circuit size, prove polynomial
generation or runtime, complete PkgC, ZeroSlack, or PCCMin, put CNF-SAT in P,
remove a project assumption, or prove `P = NP`.

## Required evidence

- Lean theorem module over arbitrary finite grouped families and circuit arities.
- Axiom audit whose expected declaration count is derived from the source.
- Generic regression covering exact candidate membership, gain soundness,
  local no-gain completeness, decoder rejection/acceptance, canonicality,
  residual descent, and every Packet branch.
- Hostile JavaScript audit rejecting invented candidates, proof-free gain
  output, globalized scan failure, fixed bounds, missing branch coverage, and
  overclaims.
- Synchronized theorem inventory, publication map, status payloads, report,
  documentation, and durable read-only workflow checks.
