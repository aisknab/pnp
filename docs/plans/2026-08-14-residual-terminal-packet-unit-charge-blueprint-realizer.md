# Residual terminal Packet unit-charge blueprint realizer plan

## Legacy anchor

Pinned manuscript Section 14, `Selector realization` and `R-ChargeSurplus`,
requires a Packet selector realizer to turn replacement data into either a
verified gain or a permitted typed failure. The current reconstruction checks
strict charge surplus only after arbitrary ledgers, weights, exact accounting,
and semantic equivalence have already been supplied.

## Unbounded abstraction

Work over arbitrary direct-wire input and output arities and arbitrary finite
explicit grouped BN6 families. A unit-charge replacement blueprint carries a
candidate implementation plus occurrence-level pairing and unmatched lists.
Its support and replacement ledgers are derived canonically from the two NAND
gate counts as `List.range` ledgers with unit weight.

## Exact theorem boundary

An executable checker must accept a blueprint exactly when its pairing
partitions every current gate occurrence, covers every replacement gate
occurrence once, leaves a nonempty unmatched remainder, and the replacement is
semantically equivalent to the current implementation. From those checks Lean
must mechanically construct the existing charge-surplus realization, derive a
genuine `StrictEquivalentGain`, and prove strict residual descent. Neither a
gate-count inequality, a charge-weight inequality, nor a gain proof may be an
input field.

The realizer then scans every original blueprint payload behind every
canonical handle in one supplied explicit selector family. Its fail-closed
outcome is either one exact source atom with the derived gain or proof that no
blueprint in that supplied family passes the validator.

## Remaining downstream blockers

The replacement blueprints, occurrence pairing, unmatched list, grouped BN6
family, and candidates remain explicit inputs. The milestone does not derive
them from terminal data, establish manuscript selector faithfulness or
compatibility, serialize a polynomial selector universe, or reinterpret local
validator rejection as `BotHN`, `BotBUD`, a lower-rank `BotSeed`, global
selector silence, or ZeroSlack. It does not close HB/rank routing, establish
encoded-size or polynomial-runtime bounds, complete PkgC or PCCMin, put CNF-SAT
in P, remove a project assumption, or prove `P = NP`.

## Required evidence

- A Lean theorem module with an executable blueprint validator, canonical
  unit-charge realization, exact source-family scan, and Packet preservation.
- A source-derived axiom audit and generic regression covering acceptance,
  rejection, charge construction, strict gain, residual descent, complete
  handle scanning, duplicate-use rejection, and empty-unmatched rejection.
- A hostile JavaScript audit rejecting assumed inequalities or gains,
  noncanonical ledgers, omitted multiplicity or semantics checks, untyped
  blocker overclaims, fixed bounds, and unconditional ZeroSlack claims.
- Synchronized theorem inventory, publication map, status payloads, report,
  documentation, and durable read-only workflow checks.
