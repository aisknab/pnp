# M171 terminal budget-envelope resolver

## Evidence-led selection

M170 constructs the complete canonical terminal support-seed universe and
gives every seed a computed semantic-minimum or strict-equivalent-gain route.
The declared residual dependency order places `BUD.BudgetResolver` after that
HResolve boundary and before the remaining no-lower composition. The next
useful bounded step is therefore to introduce a mechanically checked terminal
budget envelope and a strong search-failure sidecar without presenting an
exhaustive reference scan as the manuscript's polynomial budget dynamic
program.

## Target

For every finite direct-wire candidate, candidate-derived saturation model,
and supplied pair of natural resource caps:

1. compute feasibility from the saturated extracted NAND count, nonempty
   interface, and complete saturated-record count;
2. scan the complete canonical terminal support universe rather than a
   caller-supplied family;
3. prove every returned support is governed and satisfies the recomputed
   envelope predicate;
4. prove search failure excludes every canonical seed from that envelope;
5. classify a feasible support from actual residual slack as a semantic
   minimum or a strict equivalent gain; and
6. return a total exact, gain, or strong `NoBudget` result.

The named endpoint is
`PNP.DirectWire.terminal_budget_envelope_resolver_constructive_complete`.

## Claim boundary

The two caps are explicit inputs. Subset enumeration, candidate-derived
saturation, and reference minimization are exhaustive and may be exponential.
M171 is not the manuscript's HN/BUD grammar, BWL or budget-envelope dynamic
program, blocker dependency semantics, or polynomial BudgetResolve. It does
not complete the no-lower ledger, unconditional ZeroSlack, PCCMin exactness or
runtime, concrete SAT, the root theorem, or any project-axiom removal.

- an explicit axiom transcript for every public declaration;

- durable CI and aggregate-verifier coverage.
