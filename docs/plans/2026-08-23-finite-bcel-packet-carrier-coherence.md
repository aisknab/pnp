## Evidence-led selection

The formal status still names `Formal.ZeroSlack` as a blocker. M183 now
recomputes the finite terminal `SaturatePositive` classifier and retains the
exact successful BCEL-ready nucleus. M180 through M182 exclude a positive
Packet and constant activation for one supplied grouped BN6 family. The
report-facing `ZeroSlackCertificate`, however, still reaches that family

carrier or repeat the M183 classifier. The new certificate is parameterized by
the existing M180 certificate, so its terminal problem uses exactly M180's
candidate and model. A checked list equality binds M180's family carrier to
the M183 ready nucleus through an explicit anchor bijection.
