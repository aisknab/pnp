# M194 BN6 computed-faithfulness ZeroSlack bridge

## Evidence-led selection

M193 removes the opaque complete-silence-to-ZeroSlack callback, but its final
contradiction still accepts an arbitrary function from positive residual slack
to a faithful canonical selector. The pinned manuscript derives that selector
through a load-bearing chain: BCEL constant activation, BN6 positive-packet
classification, route-clear selector payloads, and the computed faithfulness
table. Those general Lean components already exist independently. M194 will
compose them at the PCCMin boundary and move the remaining premise earlier in
the manuscript dependency graph.

grouped terminal family, carrier-size proof, payloads, route-clear result,

construct terminal data from an arbitrary encoded input, establish
