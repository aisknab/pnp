## Exact theorem target

Add `PNP.PCCMinCheckedPacketBN6BCELActivationRoute`. Its data boundary will
bind the existing checked finite BCEL-ready certificate to the same candidate
used by the M194 Packet/HB data, with Packet anchors equal to the terminal
primitive-record type. It will not contain a
`constantActivationOfPositiveSlack` function.

Define a total classifier with four mathematical outcomes:

1. the grouped Packet carrier differs from the computed BCEL nucleus;
2. the Packet cut value differs from the computed nucleus defect;
3. the first canonical nonempty proper Packet cut has activation weight
   different from that defect; or
4. the carrier and cut value agree and every canonical nonempty proper cut has
   the required activation weight.

The fourth branch must construct M194's exact data boundary by deriving the
carrier lower bound and constant activation from the checked BCEL facts. Under
complete exact-rank selector silence it may reuse M194 to construct a
`ZeroSlackResult`. The other branches must retain their exact mismatch
evidence as a typed route; they must not be converted to ZeroSlack, a gain, or
strict descent.

The public endpoint will be
`PNP.DirectWire.pccmin_checked_packet_bn6_bcel_activation_route_or_zeroslack_checked_complete`.
It must prove that complete checked selector silence yields either zero
residual slack or one of the three exact activation-linkage obstructions. It
must be total over the arbitrary finite input data and must not accept a
caller-supplied classifier result, coherence proposition, success Boolean,
constant-activation proof, or positive-slack callback.
