# M204: PkgC restoration-coverage ambient route

## Legacy anchor and dependency edge

- Legacy anchor: the pinned manuscript's Section 11.5
  `Separating-consumer theorem`. For the first disjoint nonsingleton consumer
  pair, the finite quotient-to-full restoration graph either has complete
  exact-coordinate coverage, which must produce same-key BN4 cancellation, or
  has a Hall-deficient coordinate fibre, which must remain a concrete `Q`
  route.
- Current formal boundary: M203 computes the exact ambient BN4 remainder for
  M202's cancellation branch, but that branch is reached through an always
  total caller-supplied typed restorer. The older finite restoration-universe
  classifier separately preserves complete multiplicity coverage or an exact
  Hall deficit, but its covered branch is not connected to a constructed BN4
  cancellation subledger or M203's order-independent ambient extraction.
- Exact edge closed by M204: run the finite restoration-universe classifier,
  preserve its Hall-deficit branch literally, construct one balanced
  opposite-sign BN4 unit pair for every canonical quotient unit in the
  complete-coverage branch, and extract that constructed subledger from an
  arbitrary-order ambient BN4 ledger. Return the computed remainder with exact
  residual reduction, or retain a generated cell and proof that no exact
  ambient multiset embedding exists.

This is an arbitrary-finite theorem. It must not fix the consumer carrier,
consumer count, separating-pair size, restoration-universe size, ambient-ledger
length or order, activation key, semantic signature, transport type, candidate,
circuit, rank count, or selector count.

## Unbounded abstraction and theorem targets

For every canonical quotient unit, construct exactly two BN4 unit cells at the
nested key of its complete restoration coordinate: one positive and one
negative. Lift this construction over the quotient-unit list of a separating
pair and prove:

1. exact cell count;
2. positive and negative mass equality at every complete BN4 key;
3. empty executable residual cells at every key; and
4. zero signed mass at every key.

Package the construction with the exact
`TerminalPkgCExactCoordinateCoverage` returned by the existing finite matcher.
Do not accept a typed restorer, full-candidate function, cancellation result,
mass equality, or success Boolean from the caller.

Define an exact ambient embedding for the constructed coverage-cancellation
subledger. Reuse constructive remove-first extraction to classify every
arbitrary-order ambient ledger into exactly one of:

1. a computed remainder with exact multiset decomposition and complete
   canonical residual-ledger equality; or
2. a generated cell together with proof that no remainder can satisfy the
   exact multiset decomposition.

Define a total PkgC classifier whose outcomes are:

1. `DisjointPairsSingletonized` when no separating pair exists;
2. the exact first separating pair and a concrete
   `TerminalBN5HallDeficit`, including its forced `qRestorationHall` route and
   strict neighbourhood deficit;
3. the exact first separating pair, computed coverage-cancellation subledger,
   computed ambient remainder, exact embedding, and exact complete residual
   reduction; or
4. the exact first separating pair, computed coverage-cancellation subledger,
   generated missing cell, and proof that no exact ambient embedding exists.

The public endpoint will be
`PNP.DirectWire.terminalPkgC_restorationCoverage_ambientBN4_route_checked_complete`.

## Claim boundary and downstream blockers

The consumer system, finite restoration-coordinate universe, quotient
coordinate map, full-restoration coordinate lists, and ambient BN4 ledger
remain explicit inputs. Complete coordinate coverage proves only that the
finite equality-fibre graph has enough restoration coordinates. M204 constructs
the BN4 coordinate-level cancellation mechanically, but it does not materialize
semantic full candidates, derive the restoration universe or ambient ledger
from every valid terminal input, or prove restoration semantic adequacy.

An exact Hall deficit is a local `Q` route, not yet a verified gain or globally
rank-decreasing transition. An extracted balanced subledger preserves the
ambient residual ledger in the computed remainder; it does not prove that the
remainder is empty or that a surviving residual is contradicted. An ambient
incompatibility is exact missing integration evidence, not a completed global
route.

M204 therefore does not close complete PkgC/BN3-BN6 integration, derive HN,
BUD, or HB semantics, prove global route coverage, manuscript-level
`SaturatePositive` or `BCELReady`, unconditional `ZeroSlack`, executable
polynomial `PCCMin`, deterministic CNFSAT in P, a global gate, the eligible root
theorem, or P = NP.

No fixed progress checkpoint changes state. The risk-weighted estimate remains
35 percent, the uncertainty range remains 20 to 40 percent, and zero of five
global gates remain closed. Formal artefact coverage may change independently
when the publication row is earned.

## Stop condition

cancellation subledger without a typed restorer, if Hall failure cannot retain
the exact deficit and deterministic `Q` route, or if arbitrary-order extraction
cannot produce either an exact computed remainder or a proof of no embedding,
stop at that theorem edge. Do not replace it with a supplied restorer,
