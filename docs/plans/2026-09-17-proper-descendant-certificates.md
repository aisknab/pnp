## Unbounded target and exact input boundary

Input, output, field and gate dimensions, support size, incoming boundary width,
program length and event-list lengths are arbitrary finite data.

The public entry point has the intended shape:

```lean
def verify
    (source : WireCarrier inputs outputs fields)
    (raw : RawCertificate) :
    Option (CheckedCertificate source raw)
```

A raw certificate contains only raw support records and a raw descendant program.
Its support coordinates are decoded against the actual source's exposed word,
including every computational field. The outer computational carrier has no
additional abstract profile coordinates; this is the existing model, not a claim
that the manuscript's full profile is empty. Invalid coordinates reject.

Do not accept supplied intermediate circuits, semantic equivalence, successful
compilation, owner maps, charge totals, ordering/rank functions or acyclicity
proofs. Construct every receipt from the source and raw input.

The exact acceptance condition must be equivalent to:

1. The complete outer record list decodes.
2. The complete descendant program executes on the extracted source.
3. The retained physical exterior is nonempty.
4. The final local physical gate count is strictly smaller than the extracted
   source's count.

Literal outer compilation must be derived for every executed program; it must
not become an unexplained extra success premise in the completeness theorem.
Intermediate stages may grow. Do not introduce a boundary-width bound,
per-stage decrease, exhaustive semantic oracle or a successful-prefix fallback.
