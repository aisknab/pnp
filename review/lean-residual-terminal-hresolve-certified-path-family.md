# Hostile review: terminal HResolve certified-path family

## Accepted bounded result

The milestone composes two already verified finite kernels. A supplied
duplicate-free candidate family is greedily reduced to a maximal pairwise
H-disjoint family using exact eight-domain footprint checks. Each retained
candidate exposes an exact four-coordinate minimum over its own supplied
complete certified-path family, with semantic, frontier, block, shape, and
footprint-coherence evidence intact. Every rejected candidate exposes a
selected blocker and exact first interference route.

## Fail-closed questions

- Does the candidate carry its own expected frontier rather than sharing one
  frontier that would make distinct selections artificially collide?
- Is H-disjointness recomputed from all eight footprint domains?
- Is the path minimum computed from the candidate's list rather than accepted
  through a caller-provided flag or witness?
- Does governed-path coverage require an explicit completeness proof?
- Does every listed path agree with the candidate support and frontier?
- Does maximality retain an exact selected blocker route for every rejection?
- Are duplicate freedom and input membership both preserved?

## Explicit nonclaims

No terminal procedure constructs the candidates, paths, or footprints here.
No accepted HN grammar, grammar completeness, LN confluence, ParseOrExit,
independent leaf tightness, H0-H4 sidecar, blocker-to-HB semantics, full or
polynomial HResolve, complete no-lower ledger, unconditional ZeroSlack,
PCCMin, polynomial runtime, SAT in P, or `P = NP` follows from this result.
